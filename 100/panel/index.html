    The rules

    How we'll work

    Sit out what you're close to

    If you work with, for or alongside someone in the packet, you don't vote on them. Tell Liz and she'll note it. Everyone on this panel knows people in the field, so this will come up for all of us.

    It stays confidential

    Until the January release, that includes who's on the list, who isn't and how the votes went.

    You can't be on the list

    Serving on the panel takes you out of consideration this year.

    No fees, no sponsor say

    There's no fee to be considered, ever. Sponsors fund the work, have no say in who's on the list and never see it early.

    How the vote works. The panel votes on the final 100, and Liz chairs it with the final say. Your votes carry real weight: if most of the panel says cut someone, they're cut unless Liz has a reason she'd be comfortable putting in writing. The standard is impact first, innovation second: who is reaching people and changing how they think, and who is doing it in a way a newsroom wouldn't.
