How the Stack works

  1. 01You tell us what you use, from where your work lives to how you send invoices.
  2. 02We count. A tool's rank comes from how many members use it. Nobody pays to be on it.
  3. 03The Stack goes live on projectc.com, searchable by format, so a newcomer can see what people like them use.
  4. 04If you're in the member directory, your listing links to your stack. People who come for the tools find your work.
